# 🎬 Case File 04: The Grand Reveal

> **Mission:** Solve the case. Build the solution. Reveal your work.

You've completed your training.

You've learned web development.

You've designed your digital identity.

You've explored AI-assisted development.

Now it's time for the **Grand Reveal**.

Case File 04 is the culmination of your DevStart journey, where you'll apply what you've learned to the **DevStart Mini Hackathon** and present your solution.

---

## 🎯 Mission Objectives

By the end of this case file, you should be able to:

- Apply the skills learned throughout DevStart
- Analyse a given problem
- Develop a functional solution
- Work effectively as a team
- Document your project
- Prepare a project demonstration
- Present your solution clearly
- Reflect on your development journey

---

## 🔎 The Challenge

Participants will receive the official **DevStart Mini Hackathon case study and challenge requirements**.

Your task is to investigate the problem and develop a solution.

Remember:

> **Understand the problem before building the solution.**

---

## 🛠️ Your Mission

Work through the development process:

### 1. 🔍 Understand

Analyse the problem and identify the users' needs.

### 2. 📐 Plan

Define your solution, features, and development approach.

### 3. 💻 Build

Develop your functional prototype.

### 4. 🐛 Test

Find problems, fix bugs, and verify your solution.

### 5. 🚀 Improve

Refine your application based on testing and feedback.

### 6. 🎤 Present

Prepare your final demonstration and explain your solution.

---

## 📦 Final Submission

Your project submission should include the required materials specified in the official hackathon guidelines.

This may include:

- Project repository
- Project description
- Problem statement
- Proposed solution
- Key features
- Technologies used
- Screenshots or demo
- Live deployment, where applicable
- Team information
- Presentation materials

Refer to the official **Mini Hackathon Submission Guide** for the complete requirements.

---

## 🎤 The Grand Reveal

During the showcase, you'll present your project and explain:

### 🔎 What problem did you discover?

Explain the challenge you were given.

### 💡 What did you build?

Introduce your solution and its purpose.

### ⚙️ How does it work?

Demonstrate the key features.

### 🤖 How did you use AI?

Explain how AI assisted your development process.

### 🚀 What would you improve?

Discuss what you would add or change with more time.

---

## 🏆 Showcase

This is your opportunity to share what you've built with fellow participants, organisers, speakers, and judges.

Focus on:

**Problem → Solution → Features → Demo → Impact**

Keep your presentation clear, practical, and easy to understand.
